# icp: ideal customer profiles

orphaned research branch for sculptura ideal-customer profiling. two sides:

- `creators/` - the artists/creators sculptura most wants on the platform
- `buyers/` - the buyers sculptura expects, and how they actually shop

status: hypothesis notes from external research (oct 2026), not ruled product
decisions. verify against repo rules before porting anything into sculptura.

related but separate: the colloquial ring-sizing research (why raw size
numbers fail buyers) lives on the `sizing` branch in
`offerings/rings/sizing-reality.md`, since it is product-doc material rather
than customer profiling. buyer profiles here reference it where sizing
behavior matters.

all prose lowercase by house rule. market references use iso 3166-1 alpha-2
codes. see `sources.md` for citations.
