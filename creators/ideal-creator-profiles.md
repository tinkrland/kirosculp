# ideal sculptura creators: profiles

hypothesis profiles for the artists/creators sculptura most wants to admit,
from oct 2026 external research plus repo-ruled context (invite-gated
admission, artist-to-creator progression). not ruled product decisions.

## profile 1: the pinner (the idea-haver) - primary

scrolls pinterest (and instagram/tiktok saves) constantly, saves jewelry,
looks at a piece and thinks "god this would look good if it did this and
in this", and leaves it at that. can see the idea in their head even if
they cannot draw.

- the ruled account model names this person directly: an artist is the
  person with the ideas. this profile is who the artist-to-creator
  progression exists for. sculptura makes artists into creators
- what they have: taste, spatial imagination, the ability to hold and
  modify a design in their head. the style vocabulary research (dainty,
  chunky, maximalist axes) is literally their language
- what they lack: drawing, cad, carving, bench skills, and often any
  audience. every existing path from idea to object demands an execution
  skill they do not have; commissioning someone else means losing the
  authorship of the thing they can already see
- what sculptura offers: tessa plus paracraft as translation of the mental
  image into constrained, validated parameters. the inspo collection
  (ruled artist-side feature) is their pinterest board inside the
  platform: save, imagine the modification, then try it in studio instead
  of leaving it at that. free tier fits: ideas are abundant, execution
  is the gate
- admission fit: the hardest to verify. no portfolio, no artifacts. vgen
  invite lineage plus manual review has to read taste from how they use
  inspo and studio, not from past work. open question: what does
  admission evidence look like for someone who has never made anything
- risk: idea-havers without iteration stamina. the publication gate
  filters this naturally: taste alone does not release a listing, a
  validated release does

## profile 2: the artist with a fanbase who cannot scale commissions

the dm-economy artist: an existing audience (instagram/tiktok/fan communities) doing
commission work privately: dm-based intake, manual quotes, payment via
paypal/venmo.

- evidence: commission process pains (iterations, opaque pricing, lead-time
  unpredictability) documented industry-wide; gen z/millennial buyers now
  expect custom at accessible prices, 60% of custom jewelry buyers purchase
  online
- what they struggle with: intake chaos, scope creep, no escrow, trust
  friction with strangers, no structured sizing capture (leading to the
  wrong-size returns documented in the buyers files)
- what sculptura offers: commission flow with structured intake, escrow ->
  available -> paid out wallet model (ruled), payout rails, invitation
  admission their fans can follow
- this profile matches the ruled discretionary-listing-bump target: free
  creators with sustained sales quality and own fanbases

## profile 3: the parametric/cad-native jewelry designer

designs in cad, grasshopper, or code. already produces printable jewelry,
possibly via 3d printing services or small casting setups. the natural fit
for paracraft's parameter envelope.

- evidence: the 3d-printed jewelry market is projected to reach $3.3b by
  2030 at ~20.6% cagr (2024-2030); north america above 18% cagr through
  2033; falling printer costs keep lowering the entry bar for small studios
  (grand view research, yahoo finance)
- what they struggle with today: no owned storefront with engineered-fit
  guarantees; marketplaces treat their parametric work as static listings;
  consumers cannot customize without a custom-service conversation
- what sculptura offers them: paracraft validation, engineered fit (mm-true
  sizing), release pipeline, publication and payout gates
- admission fit: strongest candidate for the invite-first cohorts; work
  is verifiable in code/params. validates the pipeline first, but is not
  the identity of the platform

## profile 4: the etsy-refugee handmade jeweler

established seller of cast/handmade jewelry, chafing at marketplace fees and
platform changes, with an existing following.

- evidence: recurring etsy fee frustration documented through the 2022 fee
  increase (pbs), seller margins squeezed, active search for alternatives
  (ruby lane zero commission, storenvy 10% sale commission cited as options)
- what they struggle with: fees vs. discovery trade-off; weak customization
  tooling; chargebacks and buyer-protection asymmetries
- what sculptura offers: the ruled fee ladder (22% -> 8% endpoints on the
  tier hypothesis), invite-lineage admission, self-set public prices, no
  geographic labor arbitrage (availability windows only, ruled)
- watch: their work may not be parametric. paracraft targets a parameter
  envelope, so their onboarding path may differ from profiles 1 and 3

## profile 5: the fine-jewelry studio (later)

small professional workshop, hallmarking relationships, assay experience.
highest production quality, most compliance-mature. needs vienna convention
/ccm and route matrix work (security/paracraft legs) before they fit the
platform cleanly. not an early target; the manufacturing legs must be
proven first (build order: paracraft/tessa, studio, console, studiogram).

## cross-cutting creator rules from the research

- commission intake should capture: occasion, target finger(s), size and
  its source, budget ceiling, deadline. this is also the buyer-side fix for
  the size-blind proposal problem
- pricing stays the creator's own; sculptura never recommends rates (ruled
  anti-arbitrage posture)
- the creator wallet (escrow -> available -> paid out, 15th-of-month
  payouts) plus hold rules on the payout leg already shape who benefits
  most: creators with steady direct demand, not dropshippers
