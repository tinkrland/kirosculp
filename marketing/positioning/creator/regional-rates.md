# future possibility: artist-authored regional commission rates

this is an idea for a later version, not a committed feature, implemented
rule, or platform recommendation to charge a particular market more.

## the artist chooses the terms

a creator might choose different commission rates for different destination
markets, including a higher rate for a swiss shipping destination. sculptura
would apply the artist's chosen rate, not infer a fair wage, calculate what a
buyer can afford, or tell the artist which market should pay what.

this is consistent with the [free-market position](pro-artist-free-market.md):
the creator decides the offer and the commissioner decides whether to accept
it. it is not a geographic discount applied to the creator's own labor.

## use the destination already supplied in the commission flow

commissioners create an account and select the shipping destination before
commissioning. for this possible feature, that destination supplies the market.
we are not asking for a residency card, inferring nationality, or deciding
someone is swiss merely because they pay in swiss francs.

a swiss destination and a chf currency choice are distinct facts. currency
conversion also remains separate from an artist choosing a different rate.

## the intentional asymmetry

the artist can choose commission terms for the market being served. the
commissioner does not receive the artist's geography or location-coded
availability as leverage to bargain those terms downward.

this is artist-authored regional pricing, not hidden individualized pricing
by an ai bot. the applicable terms must be visible before the commissioner
agrees. destination-change behavior, rate-card structure, exceptions and
legal/provider constraints remain future design work.

these pages do not create regional pricing for standard jewelry listings or
change guest checkout for ordinary purchases. this discussion concerns
commissions and their authenticated intake.
