# creator positioning: pro-artist, free market

sculptura is a for-profit jewelry platform that is deliberately pro-artist.
those positions are compatible: the business can earn from useful infrastructure
without making a creator's economic vulnerability its competitive advantage.

our commission philosophy is not to prescribe a fair wage for someone else's
life. it is to let artists set their own terms while refusing to provide
commissioners with geographic leverage to bargain those terms downward.

## scope and truth boundary

the location, hourly-rate and regional-rate discussion here concerns
commissions. it does not replace the ordinary jewelry listing's two-way
fixed-net/fixed-retail pricing equation or introduce hourly pricing for listings.

these pages explain agreed positioning and explicitly tentative ideas. they
are not proof of implemented controls, published legal terms, or a guarantee
that nobody can infer anything about a creator from information elsewhere.

marketing explains the product; it does not implement it. studio owns geometry
and validation, and issues a versioned design release after authoritative
server-side headless openscad validation. platform owns offers, commissions,
orders and listing management. see [the creator flow](../../../studio/creators/README.md),
[commission contracts](../../../buildplan/platform/commissions.md),
[pricing](../../../operations/financial/pricing/README.md), and
[reporting](../../../console/reporting/README.md).

## a public-facing starting point

> your commission rates are yours. sculptura helps you turn your work into
> jewelry and manage the transaction, not decide what your creative labor
> should cost. commissioners choose your work and terms, not a country they
> expect to buy cheaper labor from.
