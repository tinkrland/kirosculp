# availability is not a geographic price signal

this is a deliberate anti-labor-arbitrage position for commissions, not
merely a privacy preference. hiding a creator's country while displaying
their named timezone or local workday would defeat the point.

## the ambiguity is the protection

sculptura creators need not be full-time platform freelancers. they may have
a day job, study, care responsibilities, or choose to make jewelry at night.
their response availability is when they choose to participate, not a
statement of where they live or the standard business hours of that place.

one response window could belong to a bangladeshi artist working during the
day, or a connecticut college student awake at 1:48am. the commissioner does
not need to know which. sculptura does not provide the extra labels that
would distinguish them.

this breaks the usual interpretation of active hours as local work hours.
it does not require the creator to explain why they keep that schedule.

## show communication expectations, not geographic clues

show creator-chosen response availability and relevant turnaround commitments.
do not attach country, city, named timezone, timezone offset, "local business
hours", or labels suggesting a geographically typical working day.

any rendering of an availability window must preserve that distinction:
useful to the commissioner for communication, without revealing the creator's
source timezone. scheduling embeds, public metadata and profile projections
must not restore what the visible page deliberately omits.

availability is not a live activity trail or a promise of continuous presence.
a reply at an unusual time is not grounds for asking the artist to identify
their country or justify their schedule.

## why it matters commercially

the commissioner should choose work, terms, price and communication fit,
not identify a country whose labor they expect to buy cheaply. nationality
and presumed desperation should not become bargaining leverage supplied by
the platform.

this is intentional geographic ambiguity, not a guarantee that every outside
clue is impossible to interpret. private provider and legally necessary
location records remain separate. required disclosures need careful handling;
these pages do not authorize evading legal requirements.
