# supplier provenance and private evidence

## supplier evidence

approve the actual manufacturing/supply party, relevant ownership and payment
intermediaries, alloy/process and asserted certifications. bind documentary
references to production orders and the versioned design release. update review
when suppliers, routes or evidence change. geometry safety, fineness/hallmarks,
customs/origin and financial legitimacy remain separate determinations.

recycled-metal and regional-source badges require scoped supporting evidence;
creator residence cannot substantiate metal or manufacturing origin. casting,
melting or a valid hallmark does not settle sanctions or upstream provenance.

## data boundaries

minimize and restrict identity, relationship, beneficiary and risk evidence.
prefer provider tokens/statuses and verified scoped references over copying
raw documents/biometrics into application records. specify permitted purposes,
access, retention/deletion periods, applicable legal preservation and audit needs
before collection; no retention term is invented here.

never compare raw face biometrics to routing-number geography. verified identity,
residence, nationality, bank domicile and recipient ownership are separate facts.
collect extra evidence only where the applicable requirement/risk justifies it;
never publish private location to discourage geographically targeted pricing.

access to review cases must be server-authorized and audited. public storefront,
search and analytics views must not expose compliance evidence. legally required
merchant disclosures remain a separate role/jurisdiction question, including
[quebec's requirements](../../../manufacturing/research/topics/order-routing/quebec.md).
