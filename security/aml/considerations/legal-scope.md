# legal applicability before implementation

## precious-metal dealer rules are conditional

[31 cfr part 1027](https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-31/subtitle-B/chapter-X/part-1027)
defines covered goods and dealers. the dealer definition generally involves us
business purchases exceeding usd 50,000 and gross sale proceeds exceeding usd
50,000 during the prior calendar/tax year, subject to its exceptions.

finished-goods coverage and threshold calculations have precious-metal/value
conditions. the retailer exception, purchases from non-dealers/foreign sources,
the definition of precious metal and the actual contracting/supply model matter.
do not infer coverage or exemption merely by dividing raw metal cost by retail
price. counsel must establish sculptura's role and applicable values/periods.

section 1027.210 requires a covered dealer's risk-based written program with
internal controls, a compliance officer, training and independent testing.
this is not automatic "dealer registration" caused by selling gold. do not
invent a universal usd 5,000/48-hour freeze, 30-day payout ban or obligation to
file a suspicious activity report from a screenshot. reporting duties require
their own current legal determination.

## payment custody and commercial role

review seller/principal/facilitator identity, provider-approved funds flow,
actual custody and any applicable money-transmission or escrow obligations.
a database table named escrow is not licensed escrow and does not contain cash.
provider kyc does not discharge every platform obligation; nor does a proposed
aml folder prove that a regulated program is operational.

## separate mandatory and voluntary geographic rules

ofac programs are not one country blacklist. apply relevant current programs,
restricted-person/entity and ownership rules, transaction restrictions and
permitted licenses/exemptions with authorized review. see
[ofac's explanation](https://ofac.treasury.gov/sanctions-programs-and-country-information/where-is-ofacs-country-list-what-countries-do-i-need-to-worry-about-in-terms-of-us-sanctions).

owner-directed artist policy excludes fatf high-risk call-for-action jurisdictions
and the eaeu preventive bloc. the precise eaeu observer coverage and meaning of
"from" (residence/operating/payment jurisdiction versus citizenship) require an
explicit eligibility specification before enforcement. do not infer buyer or
manufacturer bans from artist policy.

[fatf black and grey lists](https://www.fatf-gafi.org/en/countries/black-and-grey-lists.html)
are distinct. the 19 june 2026 call-for-action snapshot lists dprk, iran and
myanmar. grey-list inclusion is not that blacklist or a universal sanctions ban;
no blanket grey-list artist exclusion is authorized here. version/recheck lists
instead of treating this dated research as a permanent live registry.
