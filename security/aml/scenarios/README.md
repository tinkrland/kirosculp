# scenarios

these are defensive threat models, not findings that a particular artist has
committed an offense. no numeric detector is presented as a legal mandate.

| scenario | principal concern |
|---|---|
| [self-dealing sales](self-dealing-sales.md) | connected buyer/creator accounts disguise transfers as sales |
| [stolen payments and refund diversion](stolen-payments-and-refunds.md) | payouts leave before fraud losses or refunds are resolved |
| [account farming and proxy beneficiaries](account-farming-and-proxies.md) | many profiles conceal one controller or substitute payees |
| [sanctions and payout rerouting](sanctions-and-rerouting.md) | prohibited participants hide behind allowed accounts or another rail |
| [sourcing and false provenance](sourcing-and-provenance.md) | legitimate-looking objects obscure unsupported supplier/material claims |
| [commission and delivery manipulation](commission-and-delivery.md) | fabricated milestones unlock money without the contracted outcome |

common [considerations](../considerations/README.md) supply the safeguards and
[acceptance cases](../considerations/review-audit-and-tests.md). back to
[aml overview](../README.md).
