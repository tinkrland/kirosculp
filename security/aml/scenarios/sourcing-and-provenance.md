# supplier sourcing and false provenance

## scenario

unverified metal suppliers, substituted manufacturing routes or unsupported
recycled/origin claims can give a lawful-looking order misleading provenance.
a manufacturable mesh, physical delivery or hallmark is not evidence that every
upstream supplier, payment or sourcing claim is legitimate.

## signals and legitimate lookalikes

unexplained supplier substitutions, inconsistent order/material documents and
unverifiable claimed certifications can justify review. assay detours and
legitimate cross-border production can also create complicated paperwork;
complexity alone is not wrongdoing.

## considerations against it

bind each production order to an approved supplier/process and the immutable
release, with material/assay/dispatch evidence appropriate to the claim. assess
supplier ownership and relevant restrictions separately from creator admission.
review material or route changes before quoting/dispatch and before treating a
badge as verified. retain reference/hash/version evidence, not copied branding
as proof of certification.
