# self-dealing sales and disguised transfers

## scenario

a buyer and creator controlled by the same party, or cooperating parties, may
use apparently normal purchases to convert illicit proceeds into marketplace
payouts. a physically fulfilled order and balanced ledger do not establish that
funds or participants are legitimate. this is a plausible threat, not a verified
incident or a uniquely gold-specific mechanism.

## signals and legitimate lookalikes

repeated connected buyers, shared beneficiary identifiers, circular activity
and unusual payout urgency can warrant review in combination. a successful
launch, repeat collector, family purchase or artist buying a sample can also
produce concentration or shared-address signals. high artist margin alone is
not evidence of laundering; do not recommend lower commission rates as a control.

## considerations against it

use trusted, appropriately minimized relationship and payment evidence rather
than public-country assumptions. correlate buying, fulfillment and payout events;
apply proportionate review before external disbursement when the evidence warrants
it. retain both adverse and exculpatory evidence and a reasoned human decision.
