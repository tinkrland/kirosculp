# sanctions exposure and payout rerouting

## scenario

a restricted participant or beneficiary may use an account in an otherwise
permitted jurisdiction. trying a second payout provider after a compliance
rejection may propagate the same prohibited transfer rather than solve a
coverage problem. sanctioned parties are not confined to country-block lists.

## signals and legitimate lookalikes

current list/ownership matches, restricted intermediaries and conflicting
verified records need their own analysis. similar names, diaspora citizenship
and lawful overseas banking must not automatically become definitive matches.

## considerations against it

keep mandatory sanctions outcomes and internal admission policy separate.
resolve potential matches using authorized evidence, retain the applicable
rule/version and recheck at relevant lifecycle events. legal blocked-property
or reporting duties must not be replaced by an invented ordinary refund path.

payoneer may serve an otherwise eligible artist outside supported stripe payout
coverage, subject to actual product/corridor approval. it must not bypass a
sanctions/compliance denial from stripe, sculptura or another required reviewer.
