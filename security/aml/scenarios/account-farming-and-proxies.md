# account farming and proxy beneficiaries

## scenario

multiple apparently independent human profiles may conceal one controller,
account takeover or a beneficiary who is not the verified payee. invitation
admission and verified email/phone reduce some abuse but do not prove funds,
identity or beneficiary ownership are lawful.

## signals and legitimate lookalikes

shared payout tokens, repeated beneficiary changes and takeover evidence can
support review. shared households, schools, studios, vpn exits or service
providers can produce innocent overlaps. a foreign bank account is not itself
a proxy; passport nationality is not residence or bank domicile.

## considerations against it

require authenticated, audited changes to sensitive payout configuration;
re-establish provider-required verification and eligibility after a change.
use provider-confirmed beneficiary/ownership evidence when available, not a
fictional comparison of face biometrics to a bank routing prefix. minimize
stored identity data and protect review information from public profile queries.
