# commission milestones and delivery manipulation

## scenario

colluding or compromised accounts may mark a commission accepted/completed or
provide fabricated delivery evidence to unlock a financial release. a real
design revision or shipping label does not prove the agreed milestone occurred.

## signals and legitimate lookalikes

contradictory event timing, unverifiable fulfillment and repeated connected-party
approvals can support review. private custom briefs, fast legitimate work and
high artistic value are not by themselves suspicious or grounds to lower prices.

## considerations against it

bind the contract, approval and production order to the exact versioned release;
verify role authority and the applicable milestone evidence. studio's geometry
release gate remains separate from platform's financial release decision.
require server-side eligibility immediately before disbursement, account for
replayed/concurrent events and preserve the agreed dispute/refund process.

when an order's existing ledger/escrow transition has already become terminal,
do not reopen or rewrite it to invent an aml state. a future reviewed restriction
and reconciliation model must preserve truthful historical events.
