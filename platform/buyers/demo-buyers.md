# platform: demo buyers registry

demo buyer fixtures, split from the [creators roster](../creators/demo-roster.md)
on oct 8 2026. the same rules apply: demo-only people, not real users, and
their attributes exist to stress the buyer-side designs the way real
edge-case users would. buyers get an auth user and a `buyer_profiles` row
at seed time, and the bin is that row's id, minted at signup per the
[identity model](../identity-model.md): internal join key, no public
handle or profile in v1, readable by the buyer in their own settings and
by admins, never rendered publicly.

fixtures live in [demo-buyers.json](fixtures/demo-buyers.json) (seed
input) and [demo-buyer-accounts.json](fixtures/demo-buyer-accounts.json)
(the seeded registry, regenerated by the seed run). usernames below are
fixture-only values used for deterministic passwords; they are not
public handles.
