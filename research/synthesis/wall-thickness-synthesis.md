1. **consensus production minimum for sterling silver:**
   - the consensus production minimum wall thickness for sterling silver is 0.8 mm. this is supported by evidence from ecadcam (evid-wall-003) and jfd jewelry (evid-wall-004), both of which recommend a minimum of 0.8 mm for sterling silver.

2. **consensus for ring bands specifically:**
   - the consensus for ring bands is a minimum wall thickness of 1.0 mm. this is supported by materialise (evid-wall-001), which specifies a 1.0 mm minimum for ring bands.

3. **sources agreement and disagreement:**
   - **agreement:**
     - ecadcam (evid-wall-003) and jfd jewelry (evid-wall-004) agree on a 0.8 mm minimum for sterling silver.
     - materialise (evid-wall-001) and ecadcam (evid-wall-003) agree on a 1.0 mm minimum for ring bands.
   - **disagreement:**
     - materialise (evid-wall-001) suggests a 0.6 mm minimum for general gloss finishes, which is lower than the 0.8 mm recommended by ecadcam (evid-wall-003) and jfd jewelry (evid-wall-004).

4. **explanation of disagreements:**
   - the disagreements can be attributed to different finishing processes and specific use cases. materialise provides different values based on the type of finish (gloss vs. high gloss), which may require different structural integrity. additionally, the modality of the guidelines (requirement vs. recommendation) may influence the strictness of the values provided.
