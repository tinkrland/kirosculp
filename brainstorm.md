# icp brainstorm: who sculptura is actually for

brainstorm pass (oct 2026), building on the research profiles in
[buyers](buyers/ideal-buyer-profiles.md) and
[creators](creators/ideal-creator-profiles.md). this is synthesis against
sculptura's ruled identity, not new research. status: brainstorm, not ruled.

## the one-line icp test

sculptura serves people whose taste is specific enough that mass retail
cannot satisfy it. both sides of the marketplace pass the same test:

- buyer: she can describe a ring she wants and has never seen it in a shop
- creator: he can produce that ring as a parameterized object, not a
  one-off hand carve and not a resale

if a profile can be served by a mall jeweler or by aliexpress, it is not
the icp, no matter how much money it has.

## the metal-only constraint redraws the whole map

the research profiles assumed jewelry generally. sculptura is metal-only,
parametric, made-to-order. that single constraint re-sorts every profile:

- the classic bridal money pool (diamond solitaire, 3-months-salary
  economics) is mostly out of scope. sculptura's proposal buyer is the
  alternative-proposal buyer: plain or textured bands, promise-scale
  rings, engraved-inside pieces, the gen z anti-diamond mood. smaller
  tickets than bridal, but the blind-size pain and the occasion pressure
  are identical (see the sizing branch)
- the resale/speculation buyer is out entirely: metal-only made-to-order
  has no stone-grade story, and sculptura does not want that buyer
- the stacker is the perfect buyer: stacking is additive (multiple
  tickets), self-referential (her own system, her own sizes), and
  taste-driven (exactly what a parametric engine serves). the
  self-purchaser profile should really be named the stacker-builder
- silver matters more than the research says: gen z's sub-$250 preference
  plus precious-metal-only means the entry metal carries the whole
  funnel. if the platform silently codes gold as the real product, the
  largest buyer cohort prices itself out

## buyer side: ranked bets

1. **the stacker-builder (primary)**. self-purchaser with a system. buys
   repeatedly, knows her sizes, wants variation within a language she
   already owns. parametric sliders are her native tongue; the wishlist
   signal (ruled: redacted identity) was practically designed for her.
   the fit layer matters least for her but the mm ground truth matters
   most: she is the likeliest cross-market buyer (in/eu reading us
   listings)
2. **the commission buyer (highest engagement, unit economics to prove)**.
   wants a specific thing that does not exist. today this person dms an
   artist and suffers. sculptura's commission intake, escrow, and
   parametric iteration are exactly the fix. open question: does she buy
   through studio iterations (tessa-assisted, bounded) or free-form
   messages? the ruled boundaries say bounded
3. **the alternative-proposal buyer (highest single-ticket, highest
   risk)**. metal-only means his sculptura purchase is the band or the
   whole statement, not the stone. needs: blind-size guidance, resize or
   recompile path, buy-large strategy validation, delivery to a house
   that is not the recipient's (po-box-class bans already ruled: no
   reshippers, so careful with surprises to offices)
4. **the gift buyer (broadest, least defined)**. mother's day, birthdays,
   graduation. same blindness as the proposal buyer at lower stakes and
   lower willingness to iterate. probably served by s_m_l/unisize and
   open/adjustable families rather than parametric anything

anti-buyers (serve incidentally, never design for): the investment buyer
(metal spot-price anxiety, returns-heavy), the costume-fashion buyer
(trend-chasing, price-anchor mismatch), the dropship reseller (a buyer
profile too: buys releases to flip).

## creator side: ranked bets

1. **the artist with a fanbase who cannot scale commissions (primary)**.
   the dm-economy artist: demand exists, process is chaos. sculptura
   converts his backlog into parameterized releases. matches the ruled
   discretionary listing-bump target exactly (own fanbase, sustained
   sales). admission fits vgen-style invitation lineage. the bet:
   paracraft/creator-authored bases plus tessa assistance mean he does
   not need cad skills, only taste and the willingness to define
   constrained parameters. if that is true, the cad-free creator is the
   real unlock; if it is false, sculptura is only for profile 2
2. **the parametric-native designer (highest production quality)**.
   grasshopper/cad person already making printable jewelry. smallest
   population, strongest fit, best early proof that the release
   pipeline works. likely the first invited cohort: they validate
   paracraft before the cad-free onboarding story is proven
3. **the etsy-refugee handmade jeweler (largest population, ambiguous
   fit)**. chafing at fees, has a following, but hand-carves or casts
   static designs. sculptura's fees beat etsy only if her volume
   transfers; parametric tools may not help her at all. she is a
   storefront-and-payout win without a studio win. risk: onboarding her
   exposes whether sculptura is a marketplace with a design engine or a
   design engine with a marketplace attached
4. **the fine jewelry studio (later, unchanged)**. still gated on
   hallmarking/route legs, unchanged from the research profile

anti-creators (ruled out by existing policy, collected here): dropshippers
and resale-screenshot sellers, replica houses (ip-leak map exists for a
reason), passive upload-and-leave sellers with no human operation
(creator profiles are human-operated, ruled), eaeu-based artists and
sanctioned-market artists (admission gates, ruled), anyone expecting
geographic wage arbitrage to be visible (withheld by design, ruled).

## cross-cutting tensions worth naming

- **cold-start sequencing**: buyers need listings, creators need buyers.
  the fanbase-artist (creator bet 1) is the answer the ruled admission
  model already implies: invite creators whose audiences follow them in.
  each admitted creator imports a demand pool; the invite lineage doubles
  as a growth graph. worth making explicit as the growth thesis
- **overwhelm vs depth**: the stacker wants parametric depth, the gift
  buyer wants s_m_l simplicity. one storefront must serve both without
  the gift buyer ever seeing a slider she cannot understand. unisize and
  s_m_l enum values are the gift-buyer's shelter; keep them first-class
- **precious-metal price honesty vs accessibility**: silver carries the
  funnel; the platform cannot pretend gold is the default and still serve
  gen z. but silver also carries tarnish and cheapness perceptions.
  honest metal framing is an icp decision, not just a copy decision
- **the in-market buyer is not an edge case**: the platform's owner sits
  in in. mm-first sizing and the 2.x bangle notation trap (sizing branch)
  exist because in-market retail conventions are genuinely different.
  if in is a launch market rather than a later one, several checkout
  decisions (currency, units, sizes) rank higher
- **artists become creators through gates, buyers just buy**: the
  artist-to-creator progression is ruled; there is no buyer-side
  progression. the buyer icp should never grow tiers, trust ladders, or
  status mechanics; trust levels stay private operational classifications
  on the creator side only

## open questions this brainstorm surfaces

- is the cad-free creator real? the entire primary creator bet rests on
  paracraft + tessa making a non-cad artist productive. needs a
  prototype test before the invite-first cohorts are chosen
- does the alternative-proposal buyer need a dedicated checkout path, or
  just fit-layer copy and a recompile guarantee?
- which market is market zero? the sizing and bangle research keeps
  making in-market conventions load-bearing; if in is market zero, say so
  and rank checkout localization accordingly
- what is the minimum listing count per creator before a storefront is
  worth a buyer's trust? the tier ladder's listing caps (5-40) imply an
  answer; nobody has stated the buyer-side minimum
