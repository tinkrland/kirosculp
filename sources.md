# sources

external research collected oct 15 2026 via tavily search. sources for the
colloquial ring-sizing research live on the `sizing` branch in
offerings/research/sources.md.

## self-purchase and custom buyer demographics

- briteco via plumb club / psylis: 80% of us adults buy fine jewelry for
  themselves, millennials 86%: https://plumbclub.com/the-new-buyer-the-self-purchaser
- business of fashion state of fashion 2026: self-purchase trend +58%
  2021-2024
- sairahaz, custom jewelry buyer demographics (millennials 45%, gen z 35%,
  60% online, gen z sub-$250 preference):
  https://sairahaz.com/blogs/fine-jewelry/custom-jewelry-buyer-demographics-who-s-buying
