# quebec: provincial market obligations, not a separate customs country

researched 2026-09-30. quebec is a necessary province-specific overlay for a
canadian buyer/market case. this pass does not establish a separate quebec
precious-metal purity or hallmarking regime; absence of such a finding is not
proof that no additional rule can apply.

source identifiers resolve in [sources.json](sources.json). the underlying
[canadian federal marking and import research](europe-north-america.md) remains
separate. all provincial and route approvals remain open.

## gst/qst and collection roles

`priority-qc-import-tax-firecrawl` reports that imports into canada are generally
subject to 5% gst and property brought into quebec is generally subject to
9.975% qst, with non-taxable and other specified exceptions.

`priority-qc-qst-suppliers-firecrawl` describes registration and collection
obligations for certain suppliers outside quebec and certain platform operators.
it does not justify making every foreign physical-jewelry supplier collect qst
under the same regime. before implementation, determine:

- physical goods versus services/intangible property.
- location of goods and supplier, transaction/marketplace role and applicable
  registration system.
- border collection, vendor/platform collection or recipient reporting.
- interaction with federal courier remission and provincial arrangements.
- correct tax base, exemptions, invoicing and credit/refund handling.

do not assume the cad 40 courier tax threshold automatically settles every qst
obligation, or double-charge a tax already collected through an applicable
process. province code `QC` is required; country code `CA` alone is insufficient.
exact transaction predicates remain to be verified before quote activation.

## french product and accompanying information

`priority-qc-product-language`, the oqlf product-trademark guidance updated
29 september 2026, confirms changes effective 1 june 2025.

- information on products, packaging and accompanying documents generally
  requires french, with other languages subject to the specified presentation
  conditions and exceptions.
- a recognized non-french trademark can have an exception, but generic and
  descriptive elements must also appear in french under the new rules.
- business/product names and descriptive text are not all treated identically.
- the transition to 1 june 2027 is conditional, not a blanket holiday for goods
  newly manufactured in 2026. the guide identifies eligible earlier-manufacture
  cases and specific regulated-product cases.

jewelry labeling, material descriptions, care cards and warranty documents need
an applicable-language check. do not automatically translate artist names,
product titles or commissioned inscriptions without checking their legal scope.
creator dashboard language can remain a private preference; it does not satisfy
public-market language obligations.

## online sales and merchant identification

`priority-qc-online-contracts`, the consumer protection office's merchant guide,
requires applicable distance-sales information before the transaction, including
merchant identification/contact details, detailed product description, costs,
delivery and contract information. it also describes contract-copy duties and
cancellation remedies under specified conditions.

its merchant-contact requirements include a geographic business address. that
must be reconciled with sculptura's anti-labor-arbitrage design:

- establish who legally contracts with the consumer for each product/commission
  flow; do not guess that the creator or platform is always the merchant.
- disclose the legally required merchant information through the required
  surfaces, without gratuitously exposing a non-merchant creator's location.
- do not promise absolute geographic concealment if applicable law requires
  merchant identity/address disclosure.
- do not add upfront creator government-id collection merely because an actual
  supplier/importer or merchant has a legal registration duty.

apply jurisdiction, buyer and contract-type conditions before treating this
summary as governing every foreign transaction. cancellation, custom-made
items, delivery terms and legal warranties need a transaction-specific review.
