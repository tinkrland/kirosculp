# carrera casting

status: drafted

## finding

carrera casting (us) casts gold, silver, platinum, palladium, brass, bronze and
specialty metals, with flask investment, controlled burnout and post-cast
cleaning/inspection described on the process page. quoting and customer
onboarding run through a digifabster instance
(app.digifabster.com/carrera-casting/sign-in), meaning the quote path is
automated rather than manual email. a kyc-style customer onboarding flow exists.

## conditions and caveats

- digifabster is a saas quoting front-end, not a developer api; adapter
  feasibility (automated quote retrieval, order placement, status webhooks)
  is unverified.
- location to be pinned before outreach (site implies us operation).
