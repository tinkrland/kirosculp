# carrera casting

status: drafted

## finding

carrera casting (new york, us; precision jewelry casting since 1975) casts
across 50+ metal alloys: gold, silver, platinum, palladium, brass, bronze and
specialty metals, with no order minimums, from a single custom piece to repeat
production. accepts waxes and production-ready digital files. a file checker
flags thin walls, disconnected elements, unsupported parts and castability
concerns before production, and stone setting is available on select projects
after review. with flask investment, controlled burnout and post-cast
cleaning/inspection described on the process page. quoting and customer
onboarding run through a digifabster instance
(app.digifabster.com/carrera-casting/sign-in), meaning the quote path is
automated rather than manual email. a kyc-style customer onboarding flow exists.

## conditions and caveats

- digifabster is a saas quoting front-end, not a developer api; adapter
  feasibility (automated quote retrieval, order placement, status webhooks)
  is unverified.
- location resolved: new york based since 1975.
