# 925craft

status: drafted

## finding

925craft offers jewellery casting services specializing in precision casting
of sterling silver 925 and gold 585 components via the lost wax (investment)
process, plus rubber mould making for repeat production, with components
delivered ready for further jewellery making and finishing.

## conditions and caveats

- silver 925 + gold 585 only on the cited page: no platinum/palladium evidence
  yet. limits alloy coverage vs other candidates.
- part of the silvexcraft-linked operation (site references en.silvexcraft);
  facility geography (uk vs pl) must be pinned before route modeling.
- no public api evidence.
