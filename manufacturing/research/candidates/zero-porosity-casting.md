# zero porosity casting

status: drafted

## finding

zpc (us) casts natural wax and several prototyping resin materials, offers
castaldo liquaCast (0% shrinkage) for molding, and takes orders through a piro
erp instance (piro.zpcasting.com), meaning the order path is portal-driven
rather than email-only. mjsa member.

## conditions and caveats

- piro is a jewelry manufacturing erp, not a public api; adapter feasibility
  depends on what the portal exposes to account holders.
- alloy coverage and minimums not yet cited; needs the services pass.

## sources

- [zpc services](https://zeroporositycasting.com/services/): wax and resin casting, liquaCast molding, piro order portal, mjsa. (retrieved 2026-10-12)

## how this enters the engine

us casting-house candidate with a portal order path and explicit
castable-resin support; step 0 passes on process evidence.
