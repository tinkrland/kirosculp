# weston beamor

status: drafted

## finding

weston beamor (uk) offers the full jewellery manufacturing chain: casting
(gold casting, precision casting), settings, 3d printing, cad, bespoke
jewellery services including finishing and hallmarking, plus a client portal
(shop.westonbeamor.com/portal). it is a responsible jewellery council member
and works with smo gold. this is a one-stop uk casting house with hallmarking
in-house or in-adjacency, which serves the uk hallmarking axis directly.
