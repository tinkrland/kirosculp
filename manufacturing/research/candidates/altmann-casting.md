# altmann casting ag

status: drafted

## finding

altmann casting ag (switzerland) handles 3d modeling, casting and surface
finishing with an experienced in-house product development team, and develops
its own alloys (champagne-gold newly in range; green-gold plant shown on the
company page). it is a responsible jewellery council member.

- no public api evidence; b2b trade workflow likely.
