## finding

lukas casting house (los angeles, us) does precision lost-wax casting in:
gold 10k, 14k, 18k (white, yellow, rose), 22k yellow, 24k pure; pure and
sterling silver (regular and advanced options); 950 platinum alloyed with
ruthenium or iridium; and brass (metal included). process: pattern in castable
wax resin, investment, burnout, cast, cleanup and finishing. service scope
determines how far the project continues after casting. per-metal casting
rates are published on individual service pages.
