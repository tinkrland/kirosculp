# jewelry lukas (lukas casting house)

status: drafted

## finding

lukas casting house (los angeles, us) does precision lost-wax casting in:
gold 10k, 14k, 18k (white, yellow, rose), 22k yellow, 24k pure; pure and
sterling silver (regular and advanced options); 950 platinum alloyed with
ruthenium or iridium; and brass (metal included). process: pattern in castable
wax resin, investment, burnout, cast, cleanup and finishing. service scope
determines how far the project continues after casting. per-metal casting
rates are published on individual service pages.

## captured rate model (2026-10-12)

gold casting labor: regular $3.50 per gram plus metal and a 2% gold loss
fee; advanced $8 per gram plus metal and a 3% loss fee. advanced is not
final hand polishing; polishing, stone setting and finishing quoted
separately. platinum 950 (ruthenium or iridium): regular $6.50 per gram,
advanced $13 per gram, metal charged separately, no metal loss fee, a
platinum-iridium surcharge applies. production polishing requires at
least six pieces per model; individual hand polishing has no minimum.
