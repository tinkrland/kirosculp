# lost-wax investment casting

the primary manufacturing route for high-fidelity precious metal jewelry. a disposable pattern of the piece is surrounded by refractory investment, the pattern is burned out to leave a negative cavity, and molten metal is cast into that cavity. recovered from the sculptura.dev-research pipeline study (2026-09-11), citations inline.

## the pipeline in nine stages

### 1. pattern generation and slicing

the pattern is printed by sla, dlp, or msla in castable photopolymer resin filled with [20% to 40% microcrystalline wax](https://formlabs-media.formlabs.com/datasheets/2001505-TDS-ENUS-0.pdf). z-layer thickness ranges from [10 µm](https://cdn.webshopapp.com/shops/234164/files/356849424/1805016-gd-enus-0p.pdf) for micro-detail up to [50 µm](https://www.liqcreate.com/supportarticles/working-with-liqcreate-wax-castable-3d-printing-resin/) for general shanks. wax-filled resins solidify below 17-18°C and print at ambient 20-25°C ([liqcreate](https://www.liqcreate.com/supportarticles/working-with-liqcreate-wax-castable-3d-printing-resin/)). in sculptura this pattern is generated by paracraft from the released design, never by hand or by an assistant.

### 2. spruing and tree assembly

patterns attach to a central wax tree: main sprue [3.0 to 6.0 mm](https://www.ganoksin.com/article/sprue-system-design/) diameter, individual gates [1.5 to 2.5 mm](https://www.ganoksin.com/article/sprue-system-design/) attached at a 45° angle toward metal flow. the gate must be as thick as the thickest section of the piece so it does not freeze first and starve the casting. patterns sit [5 to 10 mm apart](https://www.ganoksin.com/article/sprue-system-design/) with at least [6 mm clearance](https://products.riogrande.com/content/Instruction-Sheets/PlastiCast-Investment-IS.pdf) from the flask wall, and the button holds [1.25x to 1.50x](https://www.ganoksin.com/article/sprue-system-design/) the liquid metal mass to feed solidification shrinkage.

### 3. investment selection and mixing

- **gypsum-bonded** (kerr satin cast 20, r&r sc20): 30-35% gypsum binder, 65-70% silica refractory, mixed at [38-40 ml water per 100 g powder](https://www.scribd.com/document/356863637/DFU-SC20-KC2000). thermal limit around 730-732°C; above that the gypsum reacts with carbon residue and [releases sulfur dioxide](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/abs/pii/S0040603103007500), pitting gold and silver castings.
- **high-expansion reinforced** (r&r plasticast, [certus prestige optima](https://formlabs-media.formlabs.com/datasheets/2001505-TDS-ENUS-0.pdf)): stronger binders that survive resin-pattern expansion. required for direct resin casting, per [r&r](https://www.ransom-randolph.com/plasticast).
- **phosphate-bonded**: for platinum's much higher temperatures ([ganoksin](https://www.ganoksin.com/article/an-overview-of-platinum-casting/)).

### 4. burnout

four phases: dewax or low ramp, pyrolysis ramp, high carbon burnout, cool down to casting temperature. total cycle: [8-10 hours for wax, 11-14 hours for resin](https://products.riogrande.com/content/Instruction-Sheets/PlastiCast-Investment-IS.pdf). the resin schedule ramps slower (2.5-3°C/min through 150-370°C), holds longer at 732°C (3-5 h versus 2-3 h), and demands kiln ventilation. full phase-by-phase table in the [resin-pattern page](resin-pattern-casting.md).

### 5. metal casting by alloy

pour temperature is melt point plus superheat; flask temperature stabilizes the mold. values from the recovered study:

| alloy | melt point | pour temp | flask temp |
|---|---|---|---|
| sterling silver 925 | 893-899°C | 960-1040°C | 480-620°C |
| 14k gold (58.5% au) | 870-900°C | 980-1060°C | 500-650°C |
| 18k gold (75.0% au) | 900-930°C | 1000-1080°C | 520-650°C |
| platinum 950 | 1768°C | 1800-1950°C | 800-950°C |

silver and gold values from [r&r](https://www.ransom-randolph.com/plasticast) and [stuller](https://www.stuller.com/articles/view/melting-and-investment-casting/); karat gold pour detail from [stuller bench tips](https://www.stuller.com/benchjeweler/resources/bencharticles/view/general-casting-tips-for-karat-golds/); platinum requires induction vacuum or centrifugal casting into phosphate investment ([ganoksin](https://www.ganoksin.com/article/an-overview-of-platinum-casting/)). casting force: vacuum assist [0.8-0.95 bar](https://www.ganoksin.com/article/wax-casting-burnout-cycles/) negative pressure, or [1.5-3.0 bar](https://www.stuller.com/articles/view/melting-and-investment-casting/) inert overpressure, or centrifugal [20-50 G](https://www.ganoksin.com/article/an-overview-of-platinum-casting/).

### 6. devesting and quenching

gold and silver flasks cool in ambient air for [12 to 20 minutes](https://www.stuller.com/benchjeweler/resources/bencharticles/view/general-casting-tips-for-karat-golds/) until the button loses its red glow, then quench in water: the thermal shock disintegrates the investment and anneals the metal. quenching earlier causes hot tearing and grain cracking.

### 7. sprue removal

gates are cut with flush shears or a diamond disk at [10,000-20,000 rpm](https://www.ganoksin.com/article/practical-guide-to-mass-finishing-jewelry/), leaving a [0.3-0.5 mm stub](https://www.ganoksin.com/article/practical-guide-to-mass-finishing-jewelry/) for rotary burr removal so the shank profile is not undercut.

### 8. mass finishing

magnetic pin tumbling with [0.3-0.5 mm stainless pins](https://www.ganoksin.com/article/practical-guide-to-mass-finishing-jewelry/) brightens recessed detail without changing dimensions; rotary stages cut for 2-4 hours in ceramic media then dry-polish 4-8 hours in walnut shell.

### 9. bench polishing and cleaning

240-600 grit leveling, tripoli pre-polish, then rouge by metal family; ultrasonic in [5-10% alkaline detergent at 50-60°C](https://www.ganoksin.com/article/practical-guide-to-mass-finishing-jewelry/), final steam at 4-6 bar (all [ganoksin](https://www.ganoksin.com/article/practical-guide-to-mass-finishing-jewelry/)). surface roughness targets: raw casting ra 1.6-3.2 µm, post-tumbling 0.8-1.2 µm, mirror finish 0.02-0.05 µm.

## published quality standards

- **dimensional tolerance:** investment casting operates at grades [ct4 to ct6 under iso 8062-3](https://www.bessercast.com/investment-casting-tolerances/): roughly plus or minus 0.10 mm (ct4) down to 0.20 mm (ct6) for jewelry-scale features.
- **assay and fineness:** gold by cupellation ([iso 11426](https://www.progold.com/standards)), silver volumetrically (iso 11427), platinum gravimetrically (iso 11210), with fineness bounds under iso 9202. these underpin the fineness claims the platform is allowed to repeat.
- **shrinkage compensation:** the investment casting institute's cumulative rule, [s total = s pattern + s metal minus investment expansion](https://knowledge.welongcasting.com/what-is-precision-casting-and-how-does-it-work): pattern 0.5-1.0%, metal 1.5-2.5% by alloy, investment expansion offsets 0.8-1.2%.

## why investment casting over direct metal printing

also recovered from the sculptura.dev production plan. direct metal printing (dmls, metal sla) exists, but loses at wearable-scale, indie-volume production:

| factor | lost wax | direct metal printing |
|---|---|---|
| surface quality | excellent after polish | grainy, needs heavy post-processing |
| metal options | almost any alloy | limited, mostly steel and titanium, few precious |
| unit cost at low volume | low | very high |
| detail at jewelry scale | excellent | good but costly |
| precious metals (au, ag) | trivial | rare and expensive |

direct metal printing earns its keep on industrial parts. for jewelry pieces, investment casting wins on price, finish, and metal selection, which is why every routed order goes through the burnout-and-pour pipeline above.

## negative findings carried over

- **no universal shrinkage scalar exists.** it varies by alloy and flask volume; paracraft must use material-specific lookups, not one global factor.
- **fast resin burnout claims are contradicted.** under-3-hour resin burnout in standard gypsum investment fails per the [formlabs](https://formlabs-media.formlabs.com/datasheets/2001505-TDS-ENUS-0.pdf) and [r&r](https://www.ransom-randolph.com/plasticast) datasheets; fast schedules require phosphate investments.
- **features under about 0.3 mm fall outside iso casting tolerance coverage.** delicate prong tips and milgrain must be flagged as bench hand work, not promised as cast.
