# the house line: platform-native templated pieces

a small, deliberately narrow catalog of dainty, stackable, high-repeat
pieces that sculptura designs and publishes itself, no external
creator involved. inspiration signal: scanning catalogs like
everyjewels' dainty stackable line (angel-number tags, sunburst
rectangle and heart pendants, puffy bubble letter/initial charms on
fine chains) shows a genre that is genuinely template-driven: one
silhouette family, one parameter (a glyph, a number, a chain length),
almost no per-piece creative decision. this is the easiest possible
paracraft product and a natural seed catalog. this is a **research
signal, not a copy mandate**: the shapes above (initial charm, number
tag, sunburst motif, small heart) are the *genre*, not a piece list to
clone.

## why sculptura, not a creator, publishes these

the creator-first rule stands: a person becomes a creator, releases
designs, and can later opt into commissions
([decision on record](../../offerings/README.md)). the house line does
not bypass that pipeline, it runs sculptura through it as a **house
creator account**: a system-owned account that publishes through the
identical release gate, the identical paracraft validation, the
identical two-way pricing math. no shortcut in the pipeline, only a
different party at the creator end.

the reason it should be sculptura and not left to organic creator
supply: these are the first few pieces the platform actually sells at
launch. a marketplace with zero listings has nothing to search, browse,
or recommend against, and dainty stackable basics are exactly the kind
of piece a new buyer expects to find immediately. the house line
covers the basics from day one (see
[stacks](../../offerings/styles/wear-context-vocabulary.jsonl) and the
["everyday fine jewelry"](../../offerings/styles/README.md) rename):
a small launch catalog of template pieces, in the genre, that already
sells while creator supply is still ramping.

the production model is the platform's own make-on-demand routing:
every house order flows through the same release-bound purchase path
as any creator listing, routed to a casting partner on demand. no
inventory, no warehousing, no upfront stock risk; the house line is
print-on-demand in exactly the sense the pod comparables
([printify, printful, gelato](comparables.md)) industrialized, but in
metal instead of merch.

## the economics: no split because there is no counterparty

when sculptura is the creator of record, there is no external party to
share revenue with, so the platform keeps the full margin above
manufacturing cost, not because the fee structure changes, but because
the "creator net earnings" side of the two-way pricing equation
collapses to sculptura's own account. the trusted pricing computation
in [operations](../operations/README.md) still runs unchanged: same
manufacturing quote, same platform fee logic, same server-side price.
only the payee is different.

scope boundary: the house line is the launch set that covers the
basics, not an evergreen house brand. it stays a handful of templates
(initial, number/date tag, one or two simple motifs), it never gets
discovery privileges over creator listings, and it does not expand
opportunistically into a creator's established territory: once creator
supply covers a genre, the house version has no ranking or visibility
edge there.

## what it needs before it can exist

- the shapes are already candidate silhouettes in
  [pendants](../../offerings/pendants/README.md) (letters, hearts,
  tags) and chains/necklaces compose over them; nothing new is needed
  in the offerings vocabulary.
- like any release, it needs the paracraft parameter envelope for each
  template (glyph set, number set, sunburst motif bounds), the studio
  release gate, and an approved finding/chain per the
  [findings model](../../offerings/findings/README.md).
- house releases are tagged into discovery exactly like creator
  releases (stacks context, everyday-fine style); no special-cased
  visibility boost beyond normal ranking.

## comparable

[custommade.com](comparables.md) sits at the opposite end of this
spectrum (fully bespoke, maker-priced, deposit-then-final-payment) and
is a useful contrast: the house line is the templated floor, creator
commissions are the bespoke ceiling, and ordinary creator listings fill
the middle.
