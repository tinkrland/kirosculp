# piercing placements: the anatomy knowledge base

this is the research the piercing hold needs before anything ships: a
placement-by-placement map of ear anatomy, the jewelry types each
placement takes, and the dimensional standards paracraft must
understand to even model the right object. this file is knowledge, not
a release switch: the [hold](README.md) on piercing-adjacent claims
stands, and nothing here approves a piercing-safe offer.

## why paracraft needs this

"earring" is not one object. a helix piece is often a small flat-back
stud or a snug hoop around the rim; a conch piece is a large stud
inside the bowl or a thick-diameter hoop around it; a daith is a
curved piece threading the innermost fold; an industrial is one long
bar through two holes. if the studio only understands "stud, hoop,
drop", it will generate geometry that is wrong for the anatomy the
buyer named. placements are therefore a first-class vocabulary axis in
the studio: choosing "helix" changes wearable depth, diameter range,
curvature, and mass constraints, not just a label.

## the placement map (candidate, from retail piercing guides)

dimensions are retail-reported typical ranges, **not** partner-verified
specs; every row needs specialist evidence before any becomes a
constraint set.

| placement | typical jewelry | gauge (retail-typical) | size ranges (retail-typical) |
|---|---|---|---|
| lobe (standard/upper) | stud, flat-back labret, hoop, huggie | 20g (0.8mm) or 18g (1.0mm) | post length 6-10mm; hoop 6-12mm |
| helix (rim cartilage) | flat-back labret, small hoop, clicker | 16g (1.2mm) typical | 5-8mm wearable/hoop diameter |
| forward helix | flat-back labret, small hoop | 16g | small diameters, front-facing ends |
| tragus / anti-tragus | flat-back labret, tiny hoop | 16g | 5-6mm |
| conch (inner/outer bowl) | large flat-back stud, thick-diameter hoop | 16g | 6-12mm |
| daith (innermost fold) | curved bar, clicker, seamless hoop | 16g (14g seen) | ~8mm curvature to clear the fold |
| rook (inner ridge) | small curved bar, small hoop | 16g | 5.5-7mm |
| snug (inner ridge, horizontal) | small curved bar | 16g | anatomy-dependent |
| industrial (scaffold, two holes) | single long straight bar | 14g (1.6mm) seen, 16g | bar length anatomy-paired, 32-40mm retail-typical |
| orbital (two rim holes, one hoop) | hoop spanning both | 16g | diameter spans both holes |

gauge chart anchor: 20g = 0.8mm, 18g = 1.0mm, 16g = 1.2mm, 14g =
1.6mm wire/post thickness.

## what this changes in the studio

- placement is a studio parameter axis alongside family: it narrows
  the object type (labret vs hoop vs curved bar), gauge, wearable
  length, curvature, and mass budget.
- the attachment interface ([findings](../findings/README.md)) is
  placement-aware: a flat back for a helix is a different spec than a
  butterfly catch for a lobe; thread-through pieces (daith clicker)
  may have no separate finding at all.
- paracraft constraint sets per placement (candidate above) must be
  evidence-backed before any placement ships: gauge tolerances, wear
  comfort, finish at contact, retention under hair/glasses/contact.
- standard lobe earrings remain the default lane; placements beyond
  the lobe are candidate vocabulary, subject to the hold.
