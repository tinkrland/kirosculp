# regional manufacturing routing

routing begins only after payment reaches the state required by console policy.

## hard filters

- design release is valid and unmodified
- partner capability record is `accepted`
- requested alloy, finish, dimensions, and process are supported
- destination is serviceable
- adapter and credentials are healthy

## scoring inputs

- landed manufacturing cost
- destination proximity
- vat, duties, customs, and tariff exposure
- turnaround and shipping estimate
- partner reliability and defect history
- insurance and claim constraints

closest is not automatically best. lowest quoted part cost is not automatically best. the selected route is the lowest-risk sensible landed outcome, and the inputs used must be recorded on the order.
