# overview

sculptura is a marketplace for digitally crafted artifacts. designers and 3d artists open small stores under sculptura.shop/yourname, list pieces with materials and prices, and buyers browse, add to cart, and place made-to-order requests.

it is built for two audiences:

- buyers who want sculptural objects, jewelry, or wearable forms produced on demand from a chosen material
- creators who want a low-friction store without setting up their own e-commerce stack

the experience is intentionally quiet. paper background, serif headlines, lowercase ui copy, lots of whitespace. the goal is to feel less like a saas dashboard and more like an editorial catalog.

every store has its own waitlist, tip jar, coupons, faq, and accent colors. orders go through a manual review and confirmation step rather than charging a card immediately, because pieces are produced after the order is placed.
